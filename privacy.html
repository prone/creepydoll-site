Creepy Doll — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Creepy Doll collects no data. None.

  • No personal information is collected, stored, or transmitted.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no network requests — the game runs entirely on your device and works offline.
  • Your progress (settings, achievements, completion) is saved only in local storage on your own device. It never leaves it, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to share, sell, or delete on our side.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/prone/creepydoll.
