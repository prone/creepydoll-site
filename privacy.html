Creepy Doll — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Creepy Doll collects no data while you play.

  • No personal information is collected, stored, or transmitted while playing.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers.
  • No accounts, no sign-in — the game runs entirely on your device and works offline.
  • Your progress (settings, achievements, completion) is saved only in local storage on your own device. It never leaves it, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

The board (optional)

When you finish the game, the win screen offers to sign the public leaderboard at creepydoll.party/scores.html. This is the only network request the game ever makes, and it happens only if you type a name and press Enter. Leave the name blank and nothing is sent.

  • What is sent: the name you typed (up to 12 characters), your score, how long the run took, how many times you died, which accolades you earned (minigames won, parts found, untouched levels, hearts), your completion percentage, and the game version.
  • Where it goes: a database hosted by Supabase. Like any web request, the hosting provider can see your IP address in transit; we do not store it alongside your run.
  • Who can see it: everyone. The board is public. Don't sign with anything you wouldn't want on a public page.
  • Removal: open an issue on GitHub (link below) with the name and score and we will delete the row.

Beyond the board, there is nothing to share, sell, or delete on our side.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/prone/creepydoll.
