A porcelain doll wakes on a moonlit road. Somewhere ahead, a boy is running.
The further she goes, the worse she looks, and the lullaby goes out of tune with her.
Tag him. That's all. Tag him and be friends.

WHAT WAITS IN THE DARK

🌙

7 LEVELS

The blood-moon road. The boy's own house. The deep woods, a snowy ascent, a tomb older than any of it, the hollow mountain beneath, and the high air above.

🦇

6 BOSS FORMS

The boy keeps changing: a bat-throwing count, a clawed werewolf, an icicle-shrugging yeti, a skull-hurling gold-masked god, a fire-breathing wyrm, and a three-headed storm king.

🎪

16 MINIGAMES

Doll toss, balloon darts, coffin shuffle, tarot pairs, bell toll, crow gallery, grave dig, glyph rites, scarab races, spear gauntlets, a minecart rush, a blind pool, a rockfall, a star catch, a weathervane, a cloud hop: the carnival follows her everywhere.

👁

LOST BUTTON EYES

Five are hidden along the road. Find four and she gets to see again. Worth it, mostly.

🕯

LANTERNS & CANDLES

Checkpoints that cost seconds, not runs. Death takes a heart; the light gives her back.

🛡

ASSIST, WITHOUT SHAME

Invincibility, game speed, infinite hearts, reduced flash, skip-minigames, all one Esc away, all saved.
