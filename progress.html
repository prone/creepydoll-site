HOW SHE GROWS

the further she goes, the worse she looks. that's the point.

There is no experience bar. Her power is her decay: the road cracks the porcelain, the house melts her, and the night hands her wings, hearts, and weapons. This page is everything she can become, and everything that scores.

THE CREEP METER

Her power is her decay. One meter, bottom-left of the HUD, filled by anything she does while she stays alive. Five notches, one every twenty points. It never resets between levels, and nothing on it is ever required to finish one. Every stage changes how she looks, reddens the moon, and detunes the music-box lullaby a little more.

What fills it. New ground walked (five points a screen). Kills (a snake is four, a valkyrie six). A minigame won, a lost part found, a boss wounded, the dog sent off: ten each. A lantern lit: three. A button eye: two.

Clean play fills it faster. Every fifteen seconds without being hurt steps the rate up, to double after a minute. One hit and the streak is gone.

A hit knocks a notch off. Twenty points, a full stage, every time something touches her. Vermin cost ten. A pit costs a heart and a notch. "she is less than she was."

Breaking empties it, but she can go back for herself. Lose her last heart and the meter is zero, but a glinting piece of her stays where she fell, holding everything she had. Retry the level, walk over it, and she is whole again. Break again before you reach it and the piece is gone for good.

THE MELT

The fifth notch, at eighty. A red gone almost black. It takes heat.

Heat melts her. Beside a candle in the house, a torch in the tomb, or an ember stone in the hollow mountain, riding the dragon, or aboard a burning saucer, the meter runs at double speed, and only there can it cross eighty. Lanterns, will-o-wisps, and frozen crystals are cold light: the road can crack her, but it can never melt her.

Cold sets her. On the mountain the meter fills at half speed and the melt drains away. She climbs the summit very wrong but solid. The tomb's torches and the mountain's embers melt her again; the high air is neither hot nor cold, and keeps her as she arrives.

The boy sees it. Reach a boss door melted and "he sees what she is": he starts the fight already wounded, and the AT HER WORST badge is hers. Finish the game melted and the board marks the run 🖤.

HEARTS: NO CEILING

She starts with five. Every heart she earns adds a new container to the HUD, in rows of ten. Ten at once earns the OVERFULL badge.

🦇

KILL A BAT

Always one heart, even at full health.

🎪

WIN A MINIGAME

Every win pays one heart. The doll toss pays one per bucket: three buckets, three hearts.

🛸

BOARD THE SAUCER

Five borrowed hearts, shown green. They go home when she leaves; every heart she owned before boarding walks out with her.

💔

WHAT COSTS ONE

Enemy contact. A pit (after a second in the dark the last lantern pulls her back). Vermin cost half. The dog's bite. On the last heart, the dark keeps her.

RIDES

🐉

THE DRAGON

How: survive one minute in a level and a purple dragon with no eyes arrives and follows her. Jump onto its back.
Powers: free flight on the arrows. Z breathes a short gust of flame. X spits a flame ball that roasts anything it touches. C hops off.
The price: spectral valkyries climb after her while she rides, up to three at a time, spear-first, tougher than bats. Dismount and they withdraw.

🛸

THE FLYING SAUCER

How: every so often a silver door full of stars appears ahead, counting down. Reach it within three seconds.
Powers: +5 borrowed hearts, free flight, Z fires laser beams, the tractor beam under the dome lifts any enemy she hovers over and X fires the passenger back out as a projectile. C bails out.
The price: two to five jet fighters scramble with missiles. Near the end of the level the saucer starts smoking: eject in time, or the explosion costs a heart.

COMPLETION %

One number behind every "% finished": 7 parts + 6 bosses + 16 minigames + 25 achievements, equally weighted. Tab on the pause screen shows the list.

EVERY WAY TO SCORE

Points carry across levels. Dying keeps the level but resets the level's score, so a clean run is worth more than a stubborn one.
